# Fleet safety

## Near misses

A near miss must be reported within 24 hours of the event. Reports are reviewed by
the Fleet Safety team every Tuesday.

## Training

Defensive driving training is refreshed every 18 months. A driver involved in a
preventable collision completes remedial training within 10 days.

## Key indicators

The safety programme tracks 4 indicators: collisions per million kilometres,
near-miss reports, hours-of-service violations and pre-trip inspection pass rate.

## Audits

An internal safety audit is completed twice a year. Findings are closed within 30
days or escalated to the VP Operations.
