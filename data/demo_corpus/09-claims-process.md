# Cargo claims

## Filing

A customer must file a claim within 9 days of delivery. The claim requires the
shipment number, three photographs of the damage, and the delivery receipt.

## Liability

Maximum liability is $2,500 per shipment unless a higher value is declared and
insured before pickup.

## Resolution

Claims are resolved within 14 business days of receiving complete documentation.
Approved claims are paid on the next weekly invoice cycle.

## Denials

A claim is denied when the damage is recorded as pre-existing at pickup or when
the delivery receipt is signed clean.
