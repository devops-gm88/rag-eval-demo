# Cold chain handling

## Temperature bands

Frozen freight is carried at -18 C or colder. Chilled freight is carried between
2 C and 4 C. Ambient freight is carried between 15 C and 25 C.

## Monitoring

Every reefer trailer carries a sensor that logs temperature every 5 minutes. The
logger stores 30 days of readings locally.

## Excursions

A temperature excursion is any reading outside the assigned band for more than 10
minutes. Three consecutive excursions quarantine the trailer and the load is
inspected before delivery. The customer is notified within 2 hours of quarantine.

## Recording

Temperature records are retained for 24 months and are attached to the shipment
file at delivery.
