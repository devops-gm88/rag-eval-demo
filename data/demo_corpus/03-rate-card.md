# Rate card

## Linehaul

The base rate for a dry van is $2.45 per mile. Reefer linehaul is $3.10 per mile.
Flatbed is quoted per load and is not part of the mileage card.

## Minimum charge

The minimum charge per shipment is $185, applied after any accessorials.

## Fuel surcharge

The fuel surcharge is recalculated every Monday at 14:00 from the weekly diesel
index. The surcharge is applied as a percentage of the linehaul rate and is shown
as a separate line on every invoice.

## Accessorials

Detention is billed at $65 per hour after the first 2 free hours. A liftgate
request adds $45 per stop.
