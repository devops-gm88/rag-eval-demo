# Driver onboarding

## Steps

Onboarding has 9 steps, from application to first solo dispatch.

## Screening

A background check takes 3 business days. A road test takes 45 minutes and is
scored by a fleet safety officer. A driver must hold a valid Class A licence and
a clean 3-year abstract.

## First month

Every new driver is paired with a buddy driver for the first 30 days. The buddy
signs off on the driver's pre-trip inspection routine before solo dispatch.

## Equipment

New drivers are issued a fuel card, a mobile device and a tablet running the
Larkspur dispatch app.
