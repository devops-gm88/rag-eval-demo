# Incident response

## Severity levels

A Sev-1 incident is a collision, a cargo loss or any event that blocks a customer
delivery. A Sev-2 incident is a delay over 4 hours or a failed temperature check.

## Response times

Sev-1 incidents must be acknowledged by the on-call dispatcher within 10 minutes.
The customer is notified within 30 minutes of acknowledgement. A post-incident
review is held within 5 business days.

## Logging

Every incident gets a record in the incident log with a unique identifier, the
shipment number, the driver, the time of acknowledgement and the resolution.

## Reporting

Monthly incident counts by severity are reported to the VP Operations.
