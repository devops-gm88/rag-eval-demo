# Dispatch policy

## Dispatch window

The dispatch desk is staffed from 05:30 to 21:00, seven days a week.

## Load assignment

Loads are assigned to drivers no later than 4 hours before the scheduled pickup.
A driver who has not checked in within 15 minutes of the assigned window is
escalated to the shift lead.

## Next-day loads

Next-day loads are released to drivers at 16:00 on the day before pickup. Drivers
may swap an assigned load with another qualified driver, but the swap must be
recorded in the dispatch log before the pickup window opens.

## Coverage

When coverage is below 90% for a route, the dispatch desk may tender the load to
partner carriers without further approval.
