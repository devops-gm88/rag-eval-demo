# Carrier and vendor approvals

## Approval stages

Carrier approval has 3 stages: document check, insurance verification and a
performance review after the first 10 loads.

## Insurance

A partner carrier must carry a minimum of $2M in commercial auto liability and
$1M in cargo coverage.

## Scorecard

Every approved carrier is scored every 6 months on on-time performance, claims
ratio and tender acceptance. A carrier below 85% on-time performance is placed on
a performance improvement plan.

## Suspension

A carrier is suspended immediately for an expired authority or a lapsed insurance
certificate.
