# Sustainability

## Electric fleet

In 2025, 38% of local mileage was electric. The company operates 12 electric vans
for final-mile delivery in the Burlington and Hamilton zones.

## Targets

The target is 60% electric local mileage by 2028 and net-zero fleet emissions by
2040. Long-haul tractors remain diesel pending charging infrastructure.

## Reporting

A carbon report is published quarterly. It covers Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions
for owned equipment only; partner carrier emissions are reported separately once
per year.

## Charging

The Burlington depot has 6 charging bays. Charging is scheduled overnight to use
off-peak electricity rates.
