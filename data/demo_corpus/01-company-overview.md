# Larkspur Freight — company overview

Larkspur Freight is a fictional freight brokerage used as synthetic test material.
It was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Burlington, Ontario. The company
operates 14 depots across Ontario and Quebec.

## Scale

The carrier owns 120 tractors and works with 60 partner carriers. It employs 340
people, of whom 190 are drivers. Revenue in the 2025 fiscal year was 48 million
Canadian dollars. Average length of haul is 410 kilometres.

## Services

Larkspur offers dry van, temperature-controlled (reefer) and flatbed brokerage.
Dry van accounts for 62% of loads, reefer 27% and flatbed 11%.

## Departments

Operations, Fleet Safety, Customer Service, Billing and Carrier Compliance. The
Billing team reports to the Chief Financial Officer; Fleet Safety reports to the
VP Operations.
