# Warehouse operations

## Dock doors

The main warehouse has 22 dock doors, of which 4 are dedicated to refrigerated
trailers.

## Appointments

Each dock appointment slot is 20 minutes. Same-day outbound freight must be
checked in by the 15:30 cut-off. Late freight rolls to the next day's first wave.

## Inventory

Cycle counts are performed weekly on Monday for the frozen zone and on Thursday
for the ambient zone. A variance over 2% triggers a full recount.

## Staffing

The dock crew works two shifts: 06:00 to 14:30 and 14:30 to 23:00.
