# Billing and payment

## Invoicing

Invoices are issued weekly on Monday and consolidated by depot. Each invoice lists
the shipment number, the linehaul rate, the fuel surcharge and any accessorials.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 21 days from the invoice date. A late payment
carries a 2% monthly fee, applied on the outstanding balance.

## Disputes

A billing dispute must be raised within 10 days of the invoice date. Disputed
lines are held while the rest of the invoice is paid.

## Currency

Invoices to Canadian customers are issued in Canadian dollars. Cross-border
shipments are invoiced in US dollars at the rate published on the invoice date.
